## Other claims to confirm

1. Ontario restricts the use of credit information in auto insurance underwriting and rating (Simrule treats DR-011 as a Level 1 fail on this basis). Confirm in Reg. 664 and the UDAP Rule.
2. Coverage lapses may be used only in the limited circumstances described in FSRA's lapse guidance (affects DR-027, DR-077).
3. Administrative licence lapses or suspensions (unpaid fines, medical) are restricted in underwriting (affects DR-094, DR-090, DR-076, DR-002).
4. The Human Rights Code insurance provision covers only age, sex, marital status, family status and disability (affects DR-007, DR-038, DR-021, DR-080, DR-090).
5. Contents of an underwriting rule filing per FSRA's Filing Guidelines for Underwriting Rules (drives the prototype completeness checklist).
6. Whether the Proposed Automobile Insurance Rating and Underwriting Supervision Guidance has since been finalised.
7. Precedent insurer names and approval years are invented for the prototype and flagged illustrative. They are not real approvals.
